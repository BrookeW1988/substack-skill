# Source: your best-performing social post → thought-leadership article

A short post that performed proved the idea resonates. Substack is where you
give it room to breathe.

## Steps

1. **Find the winner.** Look at your last week or two of posts and pick the one
   with the strongest engagement. Easiest version: open your own analytics and
   paste the winning post's text plus its link and rough numbers.

   Pick by the best IDEA for long-form, not just the most likes. A hook that
   went off but has nothing underneath it makes a thin article.

2. **Extract the core idea.** A reel or caption is compressed — one hook, one
   point. For the article, expand:
   - What's the full argument behind the hook?
   - What story or example would you tell with 800 words instead of 8 seconds?
   - What's the contrarian edge? (Thought leadership = a sharp, defensible
     opinion, not a summary of consensus.)
   - What would you say to someone who disagreed?

3. **Build the essay.** This is the most opinion-led of the four sources. Lean
   into story and take. Shape: the hook expanded → why most people get this
   wrong → your take with a real example → what to do instead → the close.
   NOT a listicle.

4. **Links block** — link the original post ("this started as a quick post
   here") plus 2 recent related pieces. Max 3. The "this started as a post"
   framing works well; it shows the throughline from short to long form.

5. **Images** — the original post's image or thumbnail works as the hero.
   PII-check it. Otherwise text-only is fine.

## Hand off

Give the writer: the winning post's text, link and stats, the expanded argument,
the contrarian angle, 2-3 links, and the PII-checked hero image.

**Plus the voice markers.** A caption is short but it's concentrated — it's them
at their most deliberate. Note the hook construction, the rhythm, the words they
chose when they only had a few. If the post was a reel, transcribe the audio too;
that's a much bigger voice sample than the caption. See
`references/substack-structure.md` § Voice comes from the source.
